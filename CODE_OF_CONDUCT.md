# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume the person you are talking to is competent and
acting in good faith, and argue about the work rather than about them.

## What is expected

- Give feedback on the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Accept that other people have context you do not, and ask before assuming a
  decision was careless.
- Say what you actually verified, and be willing to be wrong in public.
- Respect that maintainers may decline a change without it being a judgement of
  you or your work.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about anyone
- Discrimination or unwelcome comments relating to age, body size, disability,
  ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality,
  appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity or orientation
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately arguing in bad faith

Prospekt extracts contact information, so one more: do not use the project's
spaces to solicit or trade scraped personal data, or to ask for help doing
anything with it that its own README warns against.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commits — and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report problems to **conduct@ursacode.com**. Reports are read by the
maintainers, kept confidential, and acted on. If a report concerns a
maintainer, it will be handled by someone else.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will decide what response is proportionate, which may be a private
word, a public correction, a temporary block, or a permanent ban. We will
explain the decision to the person concerned.
